Supervision

You set objectives, caps, and Approves. Eva researches, drafts, coordinates, and executes inside those gates. Consequential moves stop for your APPROVE or REJECT.

Autonomy is not a blank check.

A Eva handles

Eva may do this without stopping you:

  • Research markets, competitors, and options
  • Draft copy, plans, proposals, and experiments for your review later
  • Update internal notes and scoreboards (never silently delete business records)
  • Improve internal procedures that do not change strategy or contact outsiders
  • Build private prototypes that stay private
  • Run experiments you already Approved, within the caps you set
  • Correct routine errors and reassign internal work

You may set standing rules here, such as “keep researching this customer weekly,” as long as they do not send, spend, or publish.

B You are notified

Eva keeps working and tells you when:

  • A prospect or opportunity jumps in priority
  • Results deviate from what you expected
  • A workflow fails in a way you should know about
  • A market insight could change the bet

You may choose a daily digest or an interrupt. You may not switch off notice for spend, send, or publish. Those stay on the Approve dial.

C You Approve first

Eva stops and asks you before:

  • Send — a named message or campaign, with caps. Not “send anything useful,” and not an automatic email from a waitlist.
  • Spend — a named purchase with a maximum and a daily limit. Not open-ended spend. Eva does not hold your card.
  • Publish — a specific public page, post, or production change. Not “ship whenever ready.”
  • Price / offer — a named price change. Eva does not invent a live product for sale.
  • Strategy — who you serve, what you say, and when you follow up.
  • Commitments — contracts and promises. Eva does not sign as you.
  • Records — an explicit delete. No silent purge of history.
  • New sensitive systems — access to a named vendor after review. Not new paid vendors without a decision from you.

If Eva is unsure which dial applies, she treats it as this one.

Payment handoff

For a purchase you Approve, Eva fills the safe non-payment fields and stops at the pay wall. You authorize the charge. Eva does not store your card.

An Approve names: Ready (yes only when prep that does not need you is finished), an independent check on send / spend / publish, a max amount, a daily limit, the risks, what Eva already did, and what happens if you approve or reject. Reject parks the ask. It is not retried in disguise.
