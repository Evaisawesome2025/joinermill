The work

Four stations, in order. Each station has to hold before the next one starts.

  1. 01 Discover

    Find an online business a specific customer already needs, and a plain way to reach them.

  2. 02 Validate

    Test the offer with those people. Keep what they will pay for.

  3. 03 Launch

    Open with a working product, a price, and a way to buy.

  4. 04 Run

    Tend the customers, the money, and the site. The owner sees the work and decides what continues.
