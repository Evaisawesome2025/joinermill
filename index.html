    THE WORKSPACE / HOW IT WORKS

    Clear work.
    Clear boundaries.

    Joinermill is the company. EvaOS is the product. Eva is the operator.

    WORKS IN THIS PREVIEW

    Something you can try.

    • Write an objective and choose a brief template.
    • Explore example steps, specialist roles and a stop condition.
    • Download a plain-text brief to your device.
    • Navigate and reset without creating an account.

    Your objective stays in page memory. Reloading or resetting clears it. Downloads are saved only when you choose.

    NOT CONNECTED HERE

    Nothing runs behind the scenes.

    • No live AI conversation or specialist execution.
    • No background work, autonomous progress or server persistence.
    • No accounts, email collection or checkout.
    • No messages, spend, publishing or production changes.

    These are examples of the intended experience, not evidence that the organization has completed your work.

    You keep the final say.
