    THE WORKSPACE / YOUR BRIEF

    Make the work clear.

    Your words. One bounded result. A checklist someone can review.

    Draft · only in this tab
    01 / DEFINE THE WORKALL SIX FIELDS REQUIRED

    What do you want to achieve? 12–600 characters.

    What can someone actually use? Name source material, or say what is missing. Links are text only; nothing is fetched. Maximum 2,400 characters.

    Name one concrete output and its format. Maximum 1,200 characters.

    Set the limits: time, budget, scope and actions needing approval. Maximum 1,600 characters.

    One observable check per line. 1–12 items; 3–240 characters per item. These stay unchecked until someone reviews real work. Maximum 2,000 characters.

    Say when to stop and what to do if an input or permission is missing. Maximum 1,000 characters.

    Prepared locally from your words.
    No transmission when you review.

    A USEFUL HANDOFF

    Small enough to finish.
    Clear enough to check.

    Describe the output you need and the evidence you will use to review it. Missing inputs are useful to name, too.

    This tool checks structure and length. It cannot judge whether your plan is good, safe or complete.

    Your draft stays here.

    No automatic save. You can save one copy in this browser or download your work plan, including an incomplete brief. Keep sensitive information out, especially on a shared device.

    Saving replaces the previous browser copy. It does not sync across devices.

    THE WORKSPACE / HOW IT WORKS

    Clear work.
    Clear boundaries.

    Joinermill is the company. EvaOS is the product. Eva is the operator.

    WORKS IN THIS PREVIEW

    Something you can try.

    • Write an objective and edit all six brief fields.
    • Review your own deliverable, constraints, checklist and stop rule.
    • Write a next action and list inputs still needed.
    • Download your brief or full work plan; optionally save a browser copy.
    • Optionally send a brief for a stateless format check.

    Your unsaved draft stays in this tab. Optional saving keeps one copy in this browser, with explicit restore and removal. It does not sync. Downloads include your planning notes and happen only when you choose. Only the optional format-check button sends your brief to EVAOS; that handler does not store or log its content.

    NOT AVAILABLE HERE

    Nothing runs behind the scenes.

    • No live AI conversation or specialist execution.
    • No background work, autonomous progress or server persistence.
    • No accounts, email collection or checkout.
    • No messages, spend, publishing or production changes.

    Your brief is an editable specification, not evidence of completed work. The separate examples and directory describe possible roles only.

    You keep the final say.
