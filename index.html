    WORKS IN THIS PREVIEW

    Something you can try.

    • Write an objective and edit all six brief fields.
    • Review your own deliverable, constraints, checklist and stop rule.
    • Write a next action and list inputs still needed.
    • Download your brief or full work plan; optionally save a browser copy.
    • Optionally send a brief for a stateless format check.

    Your unsaved draft stays in this tab. Optional saving keeps one copy in this browser, with explicit restore and removal. It does not sync. Downloads include your planning notes and happen only when you choose. Only the optional format-check button sends your brief to EVAOS; that handler does not store or log its content.

    NOT AVAILABLE HERE

    Nothing runs behind the scenes.

    • No live AI conversation or specialist execution.
    • No background work, autonomous progress or server persistence.
    • No accounts, email collection or checkout.
    • No messages, spend, publishing or production changes.

    Your brief is an editable specification, not evidence of completed work. The separate examples and directory describe possible roles only.

    You keep the final say.
