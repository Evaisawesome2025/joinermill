Approvals boundaries

Founding-seat packaging (plain language). Autonomy is not a blank check.

1 · Consequential freeze

Send, spend, publish/deploy, and binding price/strategy freeze until someone says yes. Demo rule: freeze is the default.

2 · Named approver

The Approver is a named owner (you), not “the system.” Eva does not self-approve money, mail, or public ship.

3 · Hard spend cap

When a spend cap is set, work that would cross it pauses. Soft warn first; hard stop at 100%. Override is explicit and yours.

Eva handles (inside gates)

Research, drafts, internal notes, private prototypes, experiments you already Approved within caps.

You are notified

Priority jumps, material deviations, workflow failures — Eva keeps working unless you intervene.
